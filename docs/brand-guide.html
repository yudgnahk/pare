The Mark
Concept
A storage disk — a circle — reduced by a single clean stroke. The chord cut references the act of paring: deliberate, surgical removal. The fresh-cut edge catches ambient light. The removed piece disappears into the dark.
Principles
Geometry only. No ornamentation, no drop shadows, no texture. One mark — the circle — one action — the cut. The icon reads clearly at 16×16 px and holds at 1024×1024.
