# Trademark policy

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers the source code. It does not grant any right to the name
**Pare** or to its logo, app icon and brand artwork.

Brand assets not covered by the MIT license:

- `scripts/icon.svg`, `scripts/PareLogo.png`, `scripts/AppIcon.icns`
- `docs/brand-guide.html`
- the logo drawn by `Sources/PareApp/Views/Components/PareBrandLogo.swift`

## What you may do

- Say that your project is "based on Pare" or "a fork of Pare".
- Use the name Pare to refer to this project, e.g. in reviews, articles and comparisons.

## What you may not do

- Ship a fork, build or derivative app named Pare, or with a name or icon that could be mistaken for it.
- Use the Pare logo or icon in your own app, website or store listing.
- Imply that your build is official or endorsed by the Pare project.

If you distribute a modified build, rename it and replace the icon and logo.

The only official builds are the ones published at https://github.com/yudgnahk/pare/releases.
Questions: open an issue on that repository.
